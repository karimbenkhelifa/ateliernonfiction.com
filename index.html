001
The Perimeter
Sound Installation — 360° Spatial Audio — 2026
+

Up to four blindfolded visitors stand at the center of an eight-speaker circle at ear height. Built from field recordings gathered across conflicts since 1998 — Kosovo, Afghanistan, Iraq, Syria, Ukraine — the work tells the story of War through listening rather than images.

Proximity, rhythm, and silence carry the emotion. Nothing shouts. The ear is the trigger; listening is the interface; imagination does the seeing.

002
In 36,000 Ways
Sensory Installation — Live Biofeedback — 2024
+

When a missile strikes, it can produce up to 36,000 shrapnels like the one the visitor holds in their hand.

Fragments collected from the front lines of southern Ukraine. Visitors stand inside a suspended cloud of several hundred fragments, frozen in the air. Spatial sound and live biofeedback compose the soundscape based on their physiological response.

Technologies used to kill and destroy, repurposed to care and feel presence.

Status

Atelier Non-Fiction is a boutique production and consulting company founded by Karim Ben Khelifa.

We make work that demands to be experienced rather than merely watched. Our practice spans kinetic and sensory installation, responsive environments, spatial sound and extended reality.

We conceive and produce original projects, and bring our process and expertise to external collaborations at any stage, from early concept through to delivery.

Based in Berlin. Working internationally.
